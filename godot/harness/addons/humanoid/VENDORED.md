`human_trait.gd` and `transform_util.gd` are vendored unmodified from
https://github.com/V-Sekai/godot-shader-motion (`shader_motion/core/humanoid/`), which
carries godot-humanoid by lox9973, Lyuma and contributors. Apache-2.0, see LICENSE.
They convert between humanoid bone rotations and Mecanim style swing-twist triplets.
